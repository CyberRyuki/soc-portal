# Incident management

> Create incidents with `python3 scripts/new_incident.py "Title" --severity High` (it picks the next number), or manually at `soc/incidents/2026/INC-2026-001` using the **[incident template](/soc/templates/incident)**. New incidents appear automatically on the SOC Dashboard.
{.is-info}

## Escalation
1. **Tier 1 analyst** — triages and opens the incident.
2. **Tier 2 analyst** — investigates and contains with approval.
3. **Security lead** — communicates and coordinates with CSIRT and management.
